# Art Asset Brief — Inner Station farewell figures

## Story
Heart of Darkness.

## Scene and narrative beat
Part III, noon departure: people emerge onto the slope while Kurtz watches from
the steamer. A woman remains by the water with arms extended after the others
recoil from the whistle. Treat these as people watching a departure, not monsters
or a supernatural ritual. The current gameplay already works with reused
distant bearer silhouettes and the woman's offscreen narration.

## Production role
Two optional transparent figure plates: bank gathering and woman's farewell.
They replace only this departure's distant silhouettes, never the approved Deck.

## Composition
- Camera/framing: existing station perspective, medium-distance figures on its slope.
- Character position: gathering on the path/river edge; woman's feet registered at the waterline.
- Negative space reserved for UI: upper left stays quiet; no figures in UI itself.
- Foreground elements: none; vessel/rail/player remain independent existing layers.
- Midground elements: restrained overlapping human silhouettes, different poses/heights.
- Background elements: transparent; reuse the current station painting without repainting it.

## Art direction
- Shared style: existing inked station/Kurtz/bearer graphic novel, strong humane silhouettes.
- Story-specific palette: soot, swamp green, muddy ochre, muted fog gray.
- Lighting source: overcast noon, softer than the Deck's cabin amber.
- Mood: grief, separation, uncertainty, pressure; no threatening fantasy spectacle.

## Character continuity
- Character: unnamed woman described in Conrad; do not invent a name or dialogue translation.
- Clothing: grounded period adornment interpreted carefully, no random fantasy accessories.
- Face/hair identifiers: coherent with existing station cast, readable at a distance.
- Pose: arms outstretched toward the receding vessel, grounded feet; alternative neutral stance.
- Expression: resolve and loss, no caricature, sexualized pose or monstrous treatment.

## Technical output
- Resolution/aspect ratio: gathering atlas 1536×512, three 512×512 clusters;
  woman atlas 1024×768, two 512×768 poses side by side.
- Transparent background: yes, clean alpha; no baked river, foliage, shadow box or text.
- Safe crop zones: full heads/hands/feet, 24px padding around silhouettes.
- Feet pivot: gathering cells (256,480); woman cells (256,730).
- Target display: woman approximately 90×135 world units on distant bank, gathering 200×110.
- Contact shadow remains scene-owned. Existing scene scale/position moves the bank as one plane.

## Forbidden
Photorealism, glossy 3D, colonial caricatures, generic fantasy tribes, exoticized
ritual, plastic skin, fake text, mixed styles, noise, European hats copied onto
the local crowd, repainting the approved Journey Deck or changing engine logic.
