# Art Asset Brief — Kurtz's night escape

## Story
Heart of Darkness, after the station revelations; no death or final voyage.

## Scene and narrative beat
The same station slowly loses its light. An empty resting place and a low trail
lead to a clearing where an exhausted man still commands attention. Human
authority, not supernatural horror. Reference: current station and Kurtz sheets.

## Production role
One aligned night station plate, one clearing plate, one transparent atlas of
four Kurtz poses and five environmental elements. Existing art is preserved.

## Composition
- Camera/framing: fixed illustrated theatre, logical 1920×1080.
- Character position: station rest (1315,780); clearing Kurtz (1100,800).
- Negative space reserved for UI: upper left 40% subdued, no focal face there.
- Foreground elements: dark vegetation framing, contact shadows, low moving fog.
- Midground elements: readable earth strip at y=680…880; no baked characters.
- Background elements: night sky, layered trees, distant modest fire, house.

## Art direction
- Shared style: inked cartoon literary graphic novel, controlled worn texture.
- Palette: soot, swamp green, fog gray, muted cool blue; restrained ember ochre.
- Lighting: dim moon through clouds and distant fire; keep footprints readable.
- Mood: quiet, material, psychological pressure. No blood, demons or glowing eyes.

## Character continuity
- Character: existing Kurtz, gaunt bald man with long angular face, pale skin.
- Clothing: ragged charcoal coat, dirty ivory shirt, worn trousers; no accessories.
- Pose: collapsed, bracing on knee, authoritative but unsteady, exhausted seated.
- Expression: intent or exhausted, never smiling or muscular.
- Followers: ordinary distant silhouettes, partly hidden; no weapons, costumes,
  horns, tribal iconography or caricature. Small gestures behind the protagonists.

## Technical output
- Plates: landscape, actual dimensions verified after generation; same registration
  as station, scaled to 1920×1080. Opaque. No UI/text/baked characters.
- Atlas: requested 1536×1024, 3×3 grid; each cell 512×341 (last row 342).
  Row 1: collapsed, bracing, dominant Kurtz. Row 2: exhausted Kurtz, empty
  stretcher/shifted blanket, crushed grass. Row 3: palm/knee marks, bent branches,
  three simple distant human silhouettes. Transparent, padded, no baked ground.
- Safe crop: all silhouettes/objects within their own cells, feet registration
  near lower center; no shadows extending into adjacent cells.

## Forbidden
Photorealism, glossy 3D, unrelated fantasy, plastic skin, gore, stereotypes,
fake text, interface arrows, baked protagonists or a redesign of old assets.

## Integration contract
Only new story manifest URLs/crops/pivots are introduced. Scene-owned effects
provide fog, transitions and shadow. No art paths or narrative state in engine.

## Delivered art and QA corrections
Plates are 1672×941, kept aligned/scaled to the established logical canvas.
The 1536×1024 RGBA atlas uses measured uneven frame heights; exact crop/pivot
data is in the story manifest. Bracing/dominant/support poses were corrected
to show bent standing legs. A final image-tool alpha pass removed background
halos rather than masking or editing old illustrations. Final generation IDs
and sources are documented in `../heart-of-darkness/KURTZ-NIGHT-ESCAPE.md`.
