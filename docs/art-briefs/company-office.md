# Art Asset Brief — Company appointment office

## Story
Heart of Darkness, Part I. Future illustration replacing only this episode's
graphic-theatre office; the approved Journey Deck remains frozen.

## Scene and narrative beat
An apparently ordinary European institution commits Marlow to an unknown river.
The invitation, colonial map, commercial secrecy and clinical scrutiny slowly
change curiosity into unease. This is not Kurtz's later report exchange.

## Production role
Layered room illustration and optional offscreen-visitor inserts. The current
episode already functions; this brief does not require a new engine or pause
development until illustration arrives.

## Composition
- Camera/framing: 1920×1080 illustrated theatre, fixed wide framing.
- Character position: approved standing Marlow, floor contact at y700–870;
  movement bounds x360–1580. Do not bake Marlow or his shadow into a background.
- Negative space reserved for UI: upper left 32% width on desktop, 39% at
  800×600, preserving the current narrow literary dialogue column.
- Foreground: restrained dark corner geometry; no obstruction of walking feet.
- Midground: contract at (870,666), recommendation at (1140,635), medical desk
  centred near (470,650), doorway/black wool at right near (1510,650).
- Background: subdued window left-centre and coloured colonial map to its
  right, roughly x1030–1320/y172–544. The map is symbolic, never fake writing.
- Interaction approach points: letter(1140,745), map(1240,770), door(1510,790),
  contract(860,745), doctor(470,745). Preserve clear floor paths between them.

## Art direction
- Shared style: inked literary graphic novel, controlled grain and silhouettes,
  coherent with approved Marlow, never photographic cutouts.
- Story-specific palette: soot, paper ochre, desaturated swamp green, fog gray.
- Lighting source: pale window illumination; paper receives restrained warm
  accents. No tropical river, flames or night moonlight in this European office.
- Mood: administrative quiet becoming disturbing; black wool and measuring
  instrument carry the pressure without supernatural effects.

## Character continuity
- Character: Marlow retains the approved atlas and walking system unchanged.
- Clothing/face/hair identifiers: inherit that existing atlas exactly.
- Pose/expression: alert, uneasy; any future doctor/knitter insert must remain
  distinct from a controllable NPC and use the same ink/colour language.
- The aunt's farewell is a remembered conversation; do not place her inside
  the Company office. Two knitters belong at the threshold, not the medical desk.

## Technical output
- Resolution/aspect ratio: logical 1920×1080; verify 1366×768 and 800×600 contain.
- Transparent background: separate props and foreground overlays yes; wall no.
- Safe crop zones: preserve map/door silhouettes and contract/medical desk;
  no text baked into art. Keep narrative captions in the current Pixi UI.
- Supply environment/ground/foreground independently so Marlow and shadows
  retain depth and walking positions. No gameplay, state or dialogue in art.

## Forbidden
Photorealism, glossy 3D, heroic colonial imagery, fake text, mixed line weights,
random accessories, plastic skin, incidental invented ethnography, tropical
backgrounds or an image that conflates this appointment with the later return.
