# Art Asset Brief — European return room

## Story
Heart of Darkness.

## Scene and narrative beat
Marlow, still convalescent after Kurtz's death, retains the packet entrusted to
him. A Company official visits to claim it. Marlow distinguishes the report
from private letters and offers only the report without its violent postscript.
This is a domestic visit, not a new Company headquarters scene. The caller is
outside the frame; no Intended, cousin or journalist appears in this episode.

## Production role
Future room background/foreground plates. Current staging is a restrained
graphic theatre built in `createEuropeReturn.ts`, with the existing approved
Marlow atlas and report prop. No unrelated illustration is substituted.

## Composition
- Camera/framing: fixed 1920×1080, contain fit at 1366×768 and 800×600.
- Character position: feet x360–1580, y700–870; initial (760,805).
- Negative space reserved for UI: upper-left x0–750, y0–540; no important detail
  behind the existing dialogue. Keep the two options readable without new UI.
- Foreground elements: dark room corners, subtle floor grain and contact shade.
- Midground elements: desk approximately x710–1300, y600–760; report at (870,666),
  private letters at (1140,635). Keep these separate
  for the spatial actions and preserve its episode-local paper-only crop.
- Background elements: window x588–874, y170–590; door x1425–1620, y230–692.
  Window implies the distant city without pretending to reproduce Brussels.

## Art direction
- Shared style: literary graphic novel, controlled ink, no photorealism.
- Story-specific palette: soot black, muted green-grey, aged paper, tobacco ochre.
- Lighting source: cool cloudy window light across the warm, worn floor/table.
- Mood: a deceptively safe room invaded by bureaucratic pressure and memory.
- Preserve the approved Marlow silhouette/line language. This scene should feel
  distinct from the river and share the compositional discipline of Metamorphosis.

## Character continuity
- Character: existing Marlow; caller kept offscreen.
- Clothing/face: existing atlas, no alternate costume or unrelated face.
- Pose: supported six-frame walking/concern, not a new illness animation.
- Expression: exhausted, alert to the documents and the visitor.

## Technical output
- Resolution/aspect ratio: 1920×1080, 16:9; background opaque, foreground transparent.
- Transparent background: foreground/desk props yes, background no.
- Safe crop zones: both resolutions retain whole composition; floor action
  anchors and feet must stay clear. No baked actor, text, papers or visitor.
- Keep existing actor, document anchors and interaction ranges. Any future
  integration belongs only to story composition, never `src/engine/`.

## Forbidden
Photorealism, glossy 3D, fake text, random portraits, modern office furniture,
copied Metamorphosis room, jungle/boat scenery presented as Europe, or a newly
visible official without approved matching art.
