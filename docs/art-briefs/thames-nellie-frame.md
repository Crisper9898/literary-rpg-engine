# Art Asset Brief — Nellie / final Thames frame

## Story
Heart of Darkness.

## Scene and narrative beat
Return from the Intended's remembered salon to the listening yacht on the Thames.
Marlow ends his tale; the Director notices the ebb; the horizon turns the river
into a continuation of the story's darkness. Quiet, maritime, European; no fire.

## Production role
Layered background, yacht deck/rail and foreground rigging. Optional future
approved listeners' silhouettes. Existing approved Marlow atlas remains unchanged.

## Composition
- Camera/framing: fixed logical 1920×1080, contain at 1366×768 and 800×600.
- Character position: Marlow entry feet (650,810), scale 1.25, atlas pivot (80,142).
- Negative space reserved for UI: upper-left 32% desktop / 39% small viewport.
- Foreground: restrained mast at x1610–1685, fine rigging and hull edge y930–1019.
- Midground: wood yacht deck, rear rail y565–669; left mooring (460,800),
  Director's listening target (1250,800), horizon interaction (970,740).
- Background: Thames water from y500; indistinct distant shore and a black bank
  of clouds, overcast sky. No palms, jungle, steamer cabin, Congo cargo or engine.

## Art direction
- Shared style: literary graphic novel; inked nautical forms and controlled texture.
- Story-specific palette: soot black, fog gray, desaturated blue/green water,
  dull ochre wood/rope. No saturated orange or photoreal composite.
- Lighting source: diffuse covered-sky reflection; cool Marlow rim/contact shadow.
- Mood: held silence, an ordinary estuary made disquieting by the completed tale.

## Character continuity
- Character: approved Marlow, same outfit/face/six-frame gait as current atlas.
- Clothing: existing dark suit and hat, without random additions.
- Face/hair identifiers: preserve approved sprite exactly.
- Pose: existing concern/idle until a separately approved meditating pose exists.
- Expression: depleted, withdrawn. Other listeners offscreen until approved art.

## Technical output
- Resolution/aspect ratio: 1920×1080 logical full scene, export separate planes.
- Transparent background: no for sky/water; yes for deck, foreground, actors.
- Safe crop zones: preserve contain composition; no accidental 4:3 crop.
- Walkable feet rectangle: x380–1540, y730–870; keep the interaction anchors fixed.
- Preserve clear water/horizon at center, mooring at left and feet contact.
- Water remains independently animated; do not bake moving ripples or UI text
  into foreground art. Cloud shading remains derived from narrative phase.

## Forbidden
Photorealism, glossy 3D, generic fantasy ships, jungle/palms, smoke/fire, random
portrait cut-outs, fake lettering, inconsistent sprite proportions, new gameplay
geometry, modification of the approved Journey Deck art or a second engine.
