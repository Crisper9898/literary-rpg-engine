# Art Asset Brief — The Intended's drawing room and mourning figure

## Story
Heart of Darkness, Part III.

## Scene and narrative beat
More than a year after Kurtz's death, Marlow returns the last private packet.
The Intended's certainty collides with what he witnessed. The room grows dark
as she asks for his last words. The playable episode is graphic theatre now;
this brief describes missing approved illustration, not an asset already made.

## Production role
Separate background plate, transparent furniture/foreground and Intended sprite.
Do not bake Marlow, papers, text or UI into the room. No random replacement PNG.

## Composition
- Camera/framing: fixed 1920×1080 room, fitted at 1366×768 and 800×600.
- Character position: Marlow walks x360–1580, feet y700–870; Intended near
  x1400,y775, just left of the right wing. Until approved she speaks offscreen.
- Negative space reserved for UI: upper-left 39% at 800×600; keep the top-left
  wall quiet. Interaction hint at bottom; no important faces behind either.
- Foreground elements: dark right curtain/door surround and lower corner shadows.
- Midground elements: closed grand piano at x300–556,y527–783, small table at
  x1018–1242,y628–765, packet anchored x1130,y650; cold marble fireplace at right.
- Background elements: three tall curtained windows centered x675,920,1165,
  from y105–665; dusk gives them muted column-like light.

## Art direction
- Shared style: inked literary graphic novel; compatible with approved Marlow.
- Story-specific palette: soot, cold fog gray, tarnished gilt, dirty ivory,
  subdued swamp green. Keep mourning black legible against the right wing.
- Lighting source: cloudy evening through three windows; cold fireplace, no fire.
- Mood: domestic grief, unshaken belief, oppressive silence; no jump-scare ghost.

## Character continuity
- Character: Kurtz's Intended, an adult woman, not a youthful fashion model.
- Clothing: plain full black mourning dress; no invented ostentatious accessories.
- Face/hair identifiers: pale face, light hair, dark eyes; no existing approved
  likeness. Approval needed before attaching a visible sprite to this content.
- Pose: hand resting over packet; later hands over face, contained grief.
- Expression: confidence in her memory, then anguish; avoid villain framing.

## Technical output
- Resolution/aspect ratio: background 1920×1080; figures/furniture independent
  transparent PNG/WebP with explicit foot/ground pivots.
- Transparent background: no for plate; yes for actor and foreground pieces.
- Safe crop zones: all interactive anchors inside x360–1580,y700–870; preserve
  walkable band, table top and right interaction. Check full fit at 800×600.

## Forbidden
Photorealism, glossy 3D, generic fantasy mansion, fashion portrait, fake text,
arbitrary collage, mixed line weights, glowing supernatural Kurtz apparition,
baked shadows that conflict with the grounded Marlow sprite.
