# Art Asset Brief — Coastal steamer and Outer Station

## Story
Heart of Darkness, Part I. Distinct from the frozen Journey Deck and Kurtz's
later Inner Station. Existing bounded graphic-theatre episode remains playable.

## Scene and narrative beat
Marlow observes a warship firing pointlessly into the coast, then lands among
discarded machinery, forced labour and dying workers. At the bright office an
immaculate accountant announces Kurtz's commercial success. Visual contrast
must expose this contradiction rather than romanticize colonial expansion.

## Production role
Two layered environment plates (coastal passage / Outer Station) and optional
human encounter inserts. No new gameplay, UI, artificial guard combat, or
redesign of the approved Deck is part of this brief.

## Composition
- Camera/framing: logical 1920×1080 wide theatre, contain at 1366×768/800×600.
- Character position: approved Marlow with foot pivot unchanged, scale1.3,
  actual feet x380–1540/y730–890. Do not bake actor or contact shadow into art.
- Negative space reserved for UI: upper-left 32% desktop/39% at 800×600; lettering is UI.
- Foreground: restrained rigging at ship-right; tree trunks at shore edges.
  Nothing obscures the walkable band or walking feet.
- Midground coast: French passenger-steamer floor roughly x207–1725/y678–953,
  railing y609–691. Offshore warship centred(1120,520), firing toward coast.
  Do not depict this as Marlow's later command or a celebratory naval battle.
- Midground shore: worn hillside path, scrap boiler near(1040,720), grove shade
  x230–840/y490–940; accountant's door x1323–1414/y568–709, light onto path.
- Background: near-black vegetation above low surf; ashore excavated slope and
  rough plank barracks, distant river/rapids. No burning Inner Station scene.
- Interaction points stay surf450790 / warship1080750 / landing1420820;
  works1040780 / grove480815 / accountant1310775 / exit1510865.

## Art direction
- Shared style: illustrated literary theatre, controlled ink/grain matching
  existing Marlow and vegetation. No photographic pasted-on people.
- Story-specific palette: soot, desaturated swamp green, dirty ochre, gray surf.
- Lighting source: oppressive daylight, humid haze; shaded trees against the
  glare and narrow light through office planks. This is not the Deck's fire.
- Mood: oppressive extraction and bureaucratic indifference; no exotic spectacle.

## Character continuity
- Character: Marlow uses the existing atlas unmodified.
- Clothing/face/hair: maintain his approved hat, suit, silhouette and footpivot.
- Pose/expression: cautious observation; no heroic combat or saviour stance.
- Swedish captain only aboard the smaller connecting steamer, accountant with
  immaculate collar/cuffs and pen. Do not substitute Russian/Kurtz portraits.
- Worker inserts need dignity, restrained gestures and no invented costume,
  caricature, speech, gore or recovery animation. Current encounters stay
  offscreen pending these assets; biscuit does not make illness disappear.

## Technical output
- Resolution/aspect ratio: 1920×1080 composition, inspect both target displays.
- Transparent background: props/foreground/optional figures yes; sky/ground no.
- Safe crop zones: exact walking band, grove and office door remain visible;
  do not include text in images. Deliver background/ground/foreground separately.
- Preserve interaction anchors and staging; substitutions belong only to this
  episode's story configuration. No engine or canonical-state changes needed.

## Forbidden
Photorealism, glossy 3D, colonial-hero imagery, random portraits, baked text,
invented ethnography, plastic skin, mismatched line weights, sentimental rescue,
reusing Kurtz's house as this station, or changing the approved Journey Deck.
