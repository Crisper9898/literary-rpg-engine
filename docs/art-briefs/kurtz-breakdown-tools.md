# Art Asset Brief — Island repair tools and private papers

## Story
Heart of Darkness, Part III.

## Scene and narrative beat
The disabled steamer is tied at an island. Marlow helps repair a bent connecting
rod and leaking cylinders while Kurtz entrusts him with papers and a photograph.
The existing boat, river, characters and approved Deck artwork are protected.
Only these small episode-local props can receive illustrated replacements.
Current staging uses a low procedural service hatch/brazier and the existing
report illustration. They support gameplay now; this brief does not block it.

## Production role
Three transparent props: service hatch/bent rod, portable charcoal forge,
and a packet tied with a worn shoe-string. No full background or UI.

## Composition
- Camera/framing: current 1920×1080 boat world, fixed framing, no new interior.
- Character position: Kurtz at (650,730); worker can approach every prop.
- Negative space reserved for UI: upper-left and upper-right stay clear.
- Foreground elements: preserve the existing gunwale; no tall machinery.
- Midground elements: hatch at (950,735), forge at (1250,800), papers at (735,730).
- Background elements: existing river, vegetation, sky and traveling fog.

## Art direction
- Shared style: inked dark cartoon/graphic novel matching the current boat plates.
- Story palette: soot, aged brass, muddy ochre, swamp green; restrained ember.
- Lighting: existing warm boat windows and dim exterior; contact shadows owned
  by composition. Forge may have localized coal light, no full-screen glow.
- Mood: exhausting practical work beside a weakening patient, not a workshop puzzle.
- Hatch must be shallow and lie in the deck's perspective. Cylinders stay below
  the floor; avoid a giant technical diagram or machinery pasted on the deck.

## Character continuity
No new character artwork. Preserve approved Marlow/deckhand motion sheets and
the existing Kurtz cot poses. Do not show a readable photographic face without
separate story approval; the private photograph can remain mostly concealed.

## Technical output
- Hatch: 384×192 RGBA; displayed about 145×70, bottom contact anchor (192,164).
- Forge: 256×256 RGBA; displayed about 110×100, feet anchor (128,230).
- Papers: 256×192 RGBA; displayed about 68×56, base anchor (128,170).
- Transparent background: yes. Keep every contour and cord within safe margins.
- Optional hatch/forge states in separate equal-size frames; anchors must match.
- No changing spatial ranges, save keys, dialogue or gameplay to fit the artwork.

## Forbidden
Photorealism, glossy 3D, fantasy machinery, giant flames, fake letters, random
portrait inserts, mixed line weights, full-background replacements or Deck redesign.
