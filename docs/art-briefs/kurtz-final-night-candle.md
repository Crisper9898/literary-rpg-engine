# Art Asset Brief — carried candle and bedside stand

## Story
Heart of Darkness.

## Scene and narrative beat
Marlow brings light to the dying Kurtz, who nevertheless speaks of darkness.
The candle is taken, carried, put beside his cot and extinguished before Marlow
leaves. Current small procedural shapes provide that interaction now. This is
an optional future art replacement, not a reason to stop the playable episode.

## Production role
Two transparent prop sprites: small brass candlestick and a separate narrow
bedside stand. Flame/light remain a controlled runtime effect, never a baked
full-screen glow. Do not generate a replacement boat, character or room.

## Composition
- Camera/framing: current boat composition in the 1920×1080 world, viewed at 1366×768 and 800×600.
- Character position: cot at (650,730), Marlow's bedside approach at (780,800).
- Negative space reserved for UI: no artwork outside the prop footprint; upper-left dialogue stays clear.
- Foreground elements: contact shadow under stand, restrained candle highlight.
- Midground elements: candlestick follows the bearer (45,-88), or stands at (705,660) above a foot at (705,730).
- Background elements: current illustrated night river and caseta are read-only.

## Art direction
- Shared style: literary graphic novel, dark ink contour, brushed aged material, readable silhouette.
- Story-specific palette: soot black, dull oxidized brass, dirty ivory wax, restrained candle amber.
- Lighting source: candle motivates local warm light; existing night/window light stays intact.
- Mood: intimate witness under pressure, no heroic halo or decorative Gothic excess.

## Character continuity
- Character: existing Marlow and Kurtz assets are protected.
- Clothing: no change.
- Face/hair identifiers: no change.
- Pose: props only; a later raised-hand illustration must be a separately scoped, approved character asset.
- Expression: no face in these assets.

## Technical output
- Resolution/aspect ratio: candle 128×192, display about 24×48 world units; stand 128×256, display about 28×76.
- Transparent background: yes, straight alpha; no baked deck or scenery.
- Safe crop zones: leave padding for the tip of the wick, full base and stand feet.
- Anchors: candle's bottom-center is its held/stand attachment; stand's bottom-center is floor contact.
- Depth: held candle just above bearer's foot depth; bedside candle/stand at the stand's floor depth.
- Replacement must preserve those footprints and anchors without changing narrative state or interactions.

## Forbidden
Photorealism, glossy 3D, random accessories, fake text, full baked lighting layer,
new character cutouts, mismatched palettes, an oversized flame or theatrical spotlight.
