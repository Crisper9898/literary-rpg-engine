# Art Asset Brief — steamer helmsman

## Story
Heart of Darkness, part II.

## Scene and narrative beat
A skilled Black crew member steers the vessel beside Marlow. During the shore
attack he falls at the wheel; his loss is a human partnership ending, not a
monster, target or anonymous enemy. The fall is animated by composition, without
blood, wound detail or gratuitous suffering.

## Production role
Transparent full-body character sprite, feet registration, separate brass wheel.

## Composition
- Camera/framing: match the existing slightly elevated three-quarter deck cast.
- Character position: both feet grounded, torso braced, hands forward at waist
  height holding an implied wheel; wheel is not baked into the sprite.
- Negative space reserved for UI: no background, caption or UI.
- Foreground elements: boots with a compact unobtrusive edge, no baked shadow.
- Midground elements: competent adult crew member, muted work shirt and trousers.
- Background elements: transparent.

## Art direction
- Shared style: the approved sailor sprite's inked cartoon graphic-novel style.
- Story-specific palette: soot, damp muted teal, dark tobacco, warm brown skin.
- Lighting source: restrained amber rim from cabin left, cold blue river fill.
- Mood: concentration and concern, never a smile or caricature.

## Character continuity
- Character: Black adult helmsman, distinct from the existing white deckhand.
- Clothing: plain rolled-sleeve work shirt, trousers, boots; no costume trophies.
- Face/hair identifiers: short dark hair, strong brows, readable serious face.
- Pose: upright but braced, bent arms near wheel, three-quarter to viewer's left.
- Expression: alert, worried concentration, dignified.

Companion fallen pose: same person and clothing, lying on his side at the wheel,
head toward viewer left and boots toward right, knees slightly bent, one relaxed
hand near his chest. Elevated three-quarter view; no wound, blood or weapon.
Deliver transparent landscape art registered to the deck contact edge. This
avoids treating a rotated standing cut-out as the final fallen silhouette.

## Technical output
- Resolution/aspect ratio: portrait source 1111×1415, displayed at 150×210
  logical pixels, feet pivot (75, 202). Companion landscape source 1698×926,
  displayed at 230×145, deck-contact pivot (115, 138). These inspected values
  are recorded in the story manifest.
- Transparent background: yes.
- Safe crop zones: preserve entire head, hands and boots.

## Forbidden
Photorealism, glossy 3D, smiles, fashion posing, racial caricature, blood, weapons,
fake text, mixed styles, excessive costume detail, baked wheel or background.
