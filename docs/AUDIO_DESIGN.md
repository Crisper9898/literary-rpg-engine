# Audio Design

## Philosophy
Atmosphere is built from music + environmental sound + silence.

Do not keep music at full volume continuously.

## Systems
The engine must support:
- music zones
- ambient layers
- positional SFX
- dialogue ducking
- crossfades
- interior/exterior mixes
- weather layers
- leitmotif events
- scene-level silence

## Example: Heart of Darkness
Deck:
- river/ocean
- wind
- wood creaks
- distant engine
- restrained score

Engine room:
- engine dominant
- reduced water
- reduced music
- metal resonance

Fog:
- minimal or absent score
- water and distant environmental cues dominant

Kurtz:
- recurring short leitmotif introduced before physical appearance
