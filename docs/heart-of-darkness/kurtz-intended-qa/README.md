## Inspection

Text, both options and controls remain fully inside each viewport; no overflow
or accidental character crop. At 800×600 the fitted room is letterboxed and the
choice captions remain single-line. The packet becomes visible only after
delivery closes; it remains visible during both replies and after restoration.
Marlow has a foot shadow and his approved scale/atlas; the UI does not hide
him, the small table or right-hand interaction. The phase dusk darkens the
windows/wall without affecting dialogue legibility. Restored question preserves
the same composition and props; no repeated arrival overlay.

The salon is restrained graphic theatre, with clear but geometric furniture.
It does not yet match the illustrated detail of the approved Journey Deck or
La metamorfosis; this is recorded as art debt, not an artistic completion claim.
The Intended speaks from the right wing and has no unapproved substitute
sprite. See [the art brief](../../art-briefs/intended-drawing-room.md).
This episode did not change either approved scene's visuals.
